Device identifiers in MQTT payloads are pseudonymised in accordance with GDPR Article 4(5). Each device's WiFi MAC address is hashed using SHA-256 (via the ESP32's on-chip mbedTLS library) and only the first 8 hexadecimal characters of the digest are published. This identifier is stable across reboots, unique per device, and non-reversible from the payload alone. The authoritative mapping between hash and physical hardware is maintained in internal documentation and is never transmitted alongside the data.
